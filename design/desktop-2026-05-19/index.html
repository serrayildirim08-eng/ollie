the thin spine

the macOS app gives the notebook a whole quiet page. a whisper-thin left spine — four glyphs, no words — is all the navigation there is. the canvas holds one focus at a calm measure, with wide warm margins. emptiness is the design. seven screens, the real v2 palette. every screen is fluid — it fills the window at any size and scales up on larger displays, so it never sits small in the middle.
