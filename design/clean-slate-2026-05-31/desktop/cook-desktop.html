Lemon Chicken Pasta

about 30 min · serves 2 · mediterranean
you have
pasta — 200 g
chicken thighs — 2
olive oil
garlic — 2 cloves
you'll need
lemon — 1+ add
parmesan+ add
how
1Boil the pasta in well-salted water until just shy of done.
2Sear the chicken in olive oil, then the garlic, until golden.
3Add a ladle of pasta water + the lemon; toss the pasta through.
4Finish with parmesan and a little more lemon to taste.
