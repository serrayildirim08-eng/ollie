Ollie · clean-slate v2 — revised

empty in style.
rich in substance.

A calm paper notebook, not a dashboard — and not a blank page. Capture stays one-thing-per-screen. But every module page now carries real, concrete info: each submodule is an expandable info-card that shows a glanceable line of live data, expands inline for a little more, and opens a detailed page on tap. Opening the app and looking is the reward — no notification required.
