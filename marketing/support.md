# support

ollie is built and maintained by a small team. there is no phone line, no live chat. email is the way in.

---

## how to reach us

**email:** support@ollie.app

## response time

best-effort within 48 hours. weekends and holidays may be slower.

if you have not heard back after 5 business days, resend the email with "resend" in the subject line and the original date in the body.

---

## what to include in your email

so we can help you faster:

- **device** — iphone model and ios version, or browser and os
- **app version** — found in settings → about → version
- **what you were doing** — the screen or module, and what you expected to happen
- **what happened instead** — error message, blank screen, slow behavior, anything
- **screenshots** — if the issue is visual
- **steps to reproduce** — if you can, write down the exact sequence to make the issue happen again
- **brain dump welcome** — if you cannot organize the above, dump the situation in plain text. we will sort it.

---

## privacy concerns and data requests

questions about ollie's data handling, opt-out requests, account deletion, gdpr access requests, ccpa requests — same email: support@ollie.app.

include:

- the email tied to your ollie account
- the request type (export, delete, opt-out of research, gdpr access, ccpa, other)
- any relevant timing context

ollie aims to acknowledge data requests within 7 days and resolve them within 30.

## crisis and safety

ollie is not a crisis service. if you are in crisis or need urgent help, please contact your local emergency services or a regional crisis line.

when ollie detects crisis-related keywords in your text, it surfaces region-specific hotline information based on the country set in your settings. that surface is informational, not a replacement for direct contact with a crisis service.

a few baseline numbers:

- **united states:** 988 (suicide and crisis lifeline, call or text)
- **united kingdom:** 116 123 (samaritans)
- **turkey:** 182 (ministry of health hotline)
- **eu, common number:** 116 111 (helplines for children) / your country's national line for adults

if you are unsure where to turn, an emergency room is always an option.

---

## what support cannot do

ollie support cannot:

- recover a forgotten passphrase
- decrypt your data if you have lost both your devices and your backups
- give medical, psychiatric, financial, or legal advice
- intervene with third-party providers (plaid, your bank, etc.) on your behalf

if your passphrase is lost, the encrypted data is unreadable. ollie has no master key.
