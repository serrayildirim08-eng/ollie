- the local data on your devices becomes unreadable

- ollie cannot help you regain access

if you use ollie on multiple devices, the same passphrase must be used everywhere. devices will sync encrypted state through ollie's server. a sync conflict (two devices editing the same module entry without syncing in between) will be resolved by last-write-wins; in rare cases, prior edits may be lost.

## what deletion means

when you delete your account:

- ollie wipes the local data on the device you triggered deletion from

- **your past research corpus contributions, if any, remain anonymized in the corpus**, because they carry no identifier linking them to you

this asymmetry is intentional and is also stated in the privacy policy. by contributing to the corpus you agree that opting out is forward-only.
